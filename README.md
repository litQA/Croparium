**Croparium**  is a practical Minecraft mod that offers players a new way to obtain resources and enriches the overall gaming experience by turning resource gathering into cultivation.

Croparium is designed around the idea of growing resources rather than simply mining or collecting them. It introduces dedicated farmland, crop-based resource production, and automation tools that make large-scale cultivation possible.

The mod follows the **Mojang EULA** and is intended for **non-commercial use only**. Reselling, reselling modified versions, or using the mod for commercial profit is strictly prohibited.

---

## Features

- **Resource Farming** – Cultivate ores, minerals, biomass, ocean resources, and other materials as crops.
- **Dedicated Farmland** – Croparium crops require their own farmland types; vanilla farmland is not sufficient.
- **Embryo Shards** – Extract shards from materials and use them to craft seeds and accelerate crop growth.
- **Reactor Core Automation** – Automatically accelerate and harvest nearby Croparium crops, with support for modules such as auto-smelting, auto-collecting, fortune, and experience.
- **Modular Expansion Design** – The base mod does not include cross-mod crops by default. Expansion mods add compatibility with other mods, while Croparium remains the required dependency.
